Privacy policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Flashcard Pro is a flashcard website that runs entirely in your browser. There is no Flashcard Pro server and no database of users. This page explains what the site uses and where your information goes.

Without signing in

Your decks, cards, photos, voice notes and study history are stored only in your own browser (IndexedDB). Nothing is sent anywhere. Clearing your browser data deletes them.

When you sign in with Google

If you choose "Sign in with Google", Google asks you to allow these permissions:

  • Your name, email address and profile picture (openid, email, profile). Used only to show who is signed in and to keep each account's cards separate on a shared device.
  • Google Drive, only files this app creates (drive.file). Used to save your library, photos and voice notes in a "Flashcard Pro" folder in your own Google Drive, and to load them again on any device. The app cannot see, read or change any other file in your Drive.

Your data goes directly between your browser and your Google account. It is never sent to the site owner or to any other server, and the site owner cannot see your cards or your Drive.

Sharing a deck

If you use "Share by link", a copy of that one deck (its cards, photos and voice notes, not your study progress) is saved in your Drive and made readable by anyone who has the link. You can stop sharing at any time from the deck's Share window, or by deleting the file in your Drive.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, no tracking, no analytics, no cookies for tracking.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of your information with anyone.
  • No use of your data to train AI models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Flashcard Pro's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other services the site loads

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, Google's sign-in script, and, only when you use "Read text from photo", a text-reading model from a public code network (jsDelivr). Those services receive normal web requests from your browser.

Removing your data

  • Delete the "Flashcard Pro" folder in your Google Drive.
  • Remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/connections.
  • Clear this site's data in your browser settings.

Contact
